# Project 01 — AeroRun Marketing Strategy: Design Brief

Approved 2026-11-23.

## Intent
A portfolio case study proving the student can think like a junior strategist at an agency:
research → strategy → implementation → measurement, with every decision defensible in an interview.

## Scope (from the brief)
Fictional product "Nike AeroRun", ₹5,999, 18–25, Indian metros, style-led, Instagram + Facebook.
15 sections: exec summary, market overview (sourced), competitor analysis (Nike/Adidas/Puma/New Balance),
3 personas, motivations & pain points, journey map, SWOT, positioning & UVP, SMART objectives,
funnel & channel strategy, content & ad strategy, ₹1,00,000 budget, KPI framework, risks, recommendations.

## Decisions
- **Framing:** ₹1L for a ₹5,999 product = a test-and-learn launch, not a profit engine. Show the math.
- **Evidence labels:** every claim tagged VERIFIED (sourced + dated), ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS, RECOMMENDATION.
  Conflicting market estimates shown as ranges. No invented survey results — a proposed survey instead.
- **Brand safety:** no swoosh, no Nike photography, no Nike typefaces. Neutral case-study design system.
  "Fictional product — independent educational simulation, not affiliated with or commissioned by Nike" on cover + footers.
  Built as local files, not published links.
- **Creative:** ad mockups (wireframe/illustrated, no real product photos) + written creative specs.

## Outputs (all in this folder)
- `AeroRun-Strategy-Report.pdf` — designed report, all 15 sections
- `AeroRun-Executive-Deck.pptx` — 11 editable slides
- `assets/` — persona sheets, journey map, competitor table, funnel diagram (PNG)
- `AeroRun-Survey.md` — proposed research questionnaire
- `AeroRun-Interview-Guide.md` — decision / alternatives / why / likely questions

## Build
HTML + CSS + inline SVG, rendered to PDF/PNG with headless Chrome. Deck via the pptx skill toolchain.
