# AeroRun: Proposed Customer Survey

Ready to paste into Google Forms. **No results exist yet.** This is the research instrument from Section 05 and Appendix A of the report.

- **Goal:** test hypotheses H1–H4 and the ₹5,999 price before any media is spent.
- **Sample:** n ≈ 150, aged 18–25. Quotas: at least 20 per city (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai) and a roughly even gender split.
- **Distribution:** college WhatsApp groups, Instagram Stories, LinkedIn. Track responses by channel.
- **Length:** about 15 minutes. Keep the concept image (an AeroRun mockup from `assets/ad-mockups.png`) on the Q7 page.
- **Ethics:** anonymous, no names or phone numbers, with a consent line at the top.

> **Intro text:** "This 15-minute survey is for a student marketing project about sneakers. It's anonymous. AeroRun is a fictional product created for the project."

---

## Section 1: About you (screener)

**Q1a. How old are you?** Under 18 *(end survey)* · 18–20 · 21–22 · 23–25 · Over 25 *(end survey)*
**Q1b. Which city do you live in?** Mumbai · Delhi NCR · Bengaluru · Hyderabad · Pune · Chennai · Other *(end survey)*
**Q1c. What best describes you?** Student · Working full-time · Working part-time while studying · Other

## Section 2: Sneaker habits

**Q2. How many pairs of sneakers have you bought in the last 12 months?** 0 · 1 · 2–3 · 4 or more
**Q3. Roughly what did you pay for your most recent pair?** Under ₹2,000 · ₹2,000–3,999 · ₹4,000–5,999 · ₹6,000–7,999 · ₹8,000–9,999 · ₹10,000+
**Q4. Where did you first see the last pair you bought?** *(choose all that apply)* Instagram Reels · Instagram feed or Stories · YouTube · A creator or influencer · Friends · In a store · Shopping app (Myntra, AJIO etc.) · Other
**Q5. Rank what matters most when you choose sneakers** (1 = most important): Style / looks · Comfort · Price · Brand · Reviews and ratings · Durability

## Section 3: Brands and concept

**Q6. If you were buying a lifestyle sneaker for ₹5,000–8,000, which brands would you consider?** *(choose all)* Nike · Adidas · Puma · New Balance · Skechers · Campus · Decathlon · Other
**Q7. [Show AeroRun concept image.] Describe this sneaker in three words.** *(short text)*
**Q8. Have you ever bought something after seeing a creator wear or review it?** Yes, sneakers · Yes, other fashion · Yes, something else · No
**Q9. Which kind of creator do you trust most for sneaker advice?** Big celebrity · Fashion or style creator · Sneaker reviewer · Friend or someone I know · I don't trust creators for this
**Q10. [Show the three ad mockups A, B and C.] Which ad would make you tap first, and why?** A · B · C · None + *(short text: why?)*

## Section 4: Price (Van Westendorp)

Ask in this order, each as a number in ₹:

**Q11.** At what price would AeroRun be **so cheap** that you'd doubt its quality?
**Q12.** At what price would it be a **bargain**, great value for money?
**Q13.** At what price would it start to feel **expensive**, but you'd still consider it?
**Q14.** At what price would it be **too expensive** to consider?

## Section 5: Trust and buying

**Q15. What stops you buying sneakers online?** *(choose all)* Worry it's fake · Can't try for fit · Returns are a hassle · Price · Delivery time · Nothing stops me
**Q16. Which would make you trust an online sneaker purchase?** *(choose all)* Official brand store · Ratings and reviews · Free size exchange · Creator I follow recommends it · Cash on delivery · Authenticity guarantee
**Q17. For a ₹6,000 sneaker, who usually pays?** I pay myself · A parent or family pays · We split it · It's a gift

## Analysis plan

| Question(s) | Analysis | Decision it informs |
|---|---|---|
| Q1c, Q17 | Split all results by student vs working, and by who pays | Persona priorities; the Kabir co-decision hypothesis |
| Q3, Q11–Q14 | Van Westendorp: plot the four cumulative curves and read the acceptable price range | Whether ₹5,999 needs value-adds or a price change |
| Q4, Q8, Q9 | Share citing creators or Reels as discovery | H1, H2: creator budget share |
| Q5, Q15, Q16 | Rank top barriers and trust devices | Landing-page trust block; ad copy |
| Q6 | Consideration-set share by brand | Validates the competitor frame in Section 03 |
| Q7, Q10 | Code open text; count angle preference | Which angle gets the most launch spend |

**Rules before you read the data:** report results as percentages *and* counts. Flag any subgroup under 30 responses as directional. Never present convenience-sample results as representative of all Indian 18–25s.
